# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- Music students, learning alone or with a teacher, who look up composers and works, download scores and organize what they are studying.
- Private music teachers who follow their students through the teacher portal (Mentor and Maestro plans): schedule, assignments, progress reports.
- Curious listeners who read about composers, eras and instruments.

Primary audience in Brazil; Portuguese first, English as well.

## Product Purpose

Opus Atlas is an open classical music encyclopedia with free public-domain scores, a place to organize one's own study (study lists, annotations, achievements) and a portal for teachers to follow their students. Reading needs no account; the free account adds the personal layer; paid plans add unlimited contributions (Plus) and the teacher portal (Mentor, Maestro).

## Positioning

An ecosystem that connects the encyclopedia, personal study and the lesson: the work page, the student's list and the teacher's assignment are the same place. It does not sell itself on the size of the catalog.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Confirmed features live in the code and in `conteudo/sobre.ts` and `conteudo/planos.ts`; what is not built yet is marked "em breve" and never presented as included (CDC art. 37).
- Community features: contributions and curiosities (XP after seven days without an upheld report), collections, annotations, reports with feedback to the reporter.
- Bilingual routes (`/` Portuguese, `/en` English).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Opus Atlas. Voice: Brazilian Portuguese, direct and warm; no emoji.
- No exact numbers in copy ("19.177 compositores" is out): the ecosystem is the message, not the count.
- Only promise what exists today.
- Restrained palette: gold and neutrals, no tinted backgrounds, no assorted colors.

## Evidence on Hand

- Public-domain portraits, engravings and instrument drawings (`public/instruments`, `public/illustrations`, with CREDITS.json).
- No testimonials, customer logos or usage statistics to quote: do not invent them.

## Product Principles

1. Free to read, always: the account adds a personal layer, never a paywall on knowledge.
2. One place for the encyclopedia, the study and the lesson.
3. Truth over hype: claims match what the system does today.
4. The community improves the catalog, and moderation answers back.
